A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Engineering with purpose.

I am a civil engineer from Nepal with an academic background in civil engineering and a strong interest in structural engineering and earthquake-resistant construction.

My interests include the seismic performance of reinforced concrete structures, structural analysis, and the development of resilient infrastructure. I am particularly interested in engineering solutions that improve the safety of buildings and communities in earthquake-prone regions.

Through academic projects and practical experience on infrastructure construction sites, I aim to continue developing my technical knowledge and contribute to better engineering practice.
